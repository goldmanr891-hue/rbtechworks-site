The fine print

Short and plain, so there are no surprises at pickup.

Turnaround

Repairs take 2–4 business days. Builds take 5–10 business days after parts arrive. Next-business-day rush service is +50% when available.

Warranty

90 days on labor, and parts carry the manufacturer's warranty. If the same problem returns within 30 days, the fix is free.

Your data

We handle your files with care, but keeping a backup is your responsibility. We can back up your data before service for a flat $40. It’s included free with Windows reinstalls and data transfers. Backups are deleted 30 days after pickup.

Quotes

We contact you before any work that would go over the quoted price.

Payment

Home repairs are paid when you pick up your device. Business work is invoiced, with payment due within 15 days. Managed plans are billed at the start of each month by card or bank transfer (ACH).

Pickup & cancellation

Devices left 60 days after we tell you they're ready are considered abandoned. Managed plans need 30 days' written notice after the first 3 months.
